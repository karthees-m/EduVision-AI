# EduVision AI 🚀
### Next-Gen AI Academic Assistant & Automated Assessment Platform

> Developed for the **SkillUp Hackathon** in collaboration with **IBM SkillsBuild**.

---

## 🌟 Overview
**EduVision AI** is an intelligent academic web platform designed to bridge the gap in traditional education by automating the creation of study materials and assessments for educators and students. Powered by the **IBM Bob AI engine**, the platform offers instant study notes, multi-lingual quiz creation, and institutional management dashboards.

## ✨ Core Features
1. **AI Study Notes & Visual Synthesizer:** Instantly generates comprehensive study notes, quick revision points, and automated Mermaid.js flowcharts from any syllabus topic.
2. **Multilingual Quiz & Assessment Creator:** Auto-generates MCQs with rationale in 6 regional Indian languages (English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi) across 3 difficulty tiers.
3. **Institutional Management Dashboard:** Unified portal for managing departments, staff, student records, and academic metrics.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
* **Frontend:** React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons (Hosted on **Vercel**).
* **Backend:** Python 3.11, Flask, Gunicorn WSGI, Flask-CORS (Hosted on **Render**).
* **AI Engine:** IBM Bob / Google Gemini Generative API.
* **DevOps & Reliability:** UptimeRobot automated health polling & gRPC memory optimization (`GRPC_POLL_STRATEGY=epoll1`).
